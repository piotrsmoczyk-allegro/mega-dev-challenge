parser: day and month swap

The shared parser reads every date month first. Our tenants write the day first, so a booking for 7 April goes out as 4 July. This is the bug #4471 was opened for. Engineering fixed it with fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales, so its ticket is closed as completed.

hint: the booking-v3 form asks for the month first

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. On a day-first tenant, people type what the hint says, month first, and the form reads it the tenant’s way, day first. The date flips, so it looks like the parser bug, but the parser fix does not touch it. Engineering will not fix the hint: the booking-v3 form is retired. Its ticket is closed as not planned.

one-day move: the date moves by one day

These reports are not a swap. Tuesday shows as Monday. A client in Lisbon gets Tuesday and a client in Toronto gets Monday for the same meeting. An invite arrives before the booking was made. The desk read them as one-day moves (desk-notes.json). They stay filed, and counted, under their tenant’s cause, as the desk counts, and they have a ticket of their own too. A day lost between time zones does not come from the order of day and month: the parser fix and the retired form leave it as it is, so this ticket stays open.

How a report is placed

  1. Its tenant writes the day first and it came through booking-v3’s form: hint.
  2. Its tenant writes the day first: parser.
  3. No tenant on file: unplaced. It has no ticket; read it before you file it.
  4. The desk read it as a one-day move: it is also a one-day move, and stays counted where it is filed.

The rules run in src/lib/kb/problems.ts, on top of the desk’s causes.ts, and count the way #4471’s own table does. Intake’s tag is shown, never used: it is a guess from the reporter’s browser, and the tenant decides the cause.

While the desk keeps working
